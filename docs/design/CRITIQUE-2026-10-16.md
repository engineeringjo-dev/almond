## Assessment A — design review

### Assessment A: Design review of the Almond members' app (Arabic, 390×844)

Evidence: the provided shots plus my own fresh-context captures (redeem confirm and done, cart after the combo, onboarding ×3, order-type sheet, empty vouchers, empty cart, full item sheet, croissant search, Home after a 9 s wait). Scratch files are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/0c129301-b326-57a5-8c61-e057952bf43c/scratchpad/critique/a/`. Nothing in the repo was touched. I started no long-running process; every Playwright browser was closed by its script.

Note on `home-tall.png`: its empty photo plates were a capture-timing artefact. After 9 s the photographs are there. The real finding is that the photos arrive late and there is no placeholder.

#### 1. Design specificity verdict: interchangeable with any coffee or loyalty app

The structure is fine for an app people use to get a task done: greeting, black balance card, photo tiles, carousel, nudges, a five-tab bar with a raised QR button, a settings list. It is the Starbucks and Al Ameed pattern on purpose. The anonymity comes from the fact that nothing inside that structure belongs to Almond. Swap the 34pt logo badge and Dunkin' could ship it.

What reads as "AI-made" to an ordinary customer:
- **Stock line icons are the only illustration.** They are Lucide icons in grey circles or squares: onboarding (a mug, a clock and coins, each in an outline circle), the empty cart (a supermarket trolley under «كوبك ينتظرك»), empty vouchers (a gift), the More rows, the Rewards tiles and the Home nudges. This look is the signature of an app template.
- **One grey rounded slab, repeated down Home.** The order tile, six carousel slides, «طلبك المعتاد» and two nudges all share the same fill, radius and weight. There is no rhythm, no texture and no image-led moment.
- **The app explains its rules instead of having a voice.** Examples: «يتجدّد الباركود تلقائياً ما دامت هذه الشاشة مفتوحة», «اصرف 8.000 د.أ إضافية لتصبح نقاطك ×2 على كل طلب», «مثلاً: امريكانو ساخن + بلوبيري مفن». The vocabulary is a bank's: كاش باك، رصيد، استبدل، قسائم، فئتك.
- **Tiers are named «2% / 4% / 6%».** The chip «2% ☕» on the card reads as a discount sticker, not as membership.
- **The cart drops the photographs.** It shows a cake-slice glyph where Home showed the muffin you just added (cart-combo-tall).

Missed chances, all of them things the brand already owns:
- **The cups carry Almond's own doodle language** (croissant, cake slice, coffee pot, music note, book, «FRESH»). It is visible in every product photo (item-sheet.png) and appears nowhere in the interface.
- **The logo's four drawings stand for drink, food, music and reading.** The app expresses only drink and food, and only through stock icons. Music and books, the "coffee house" part that fits Amman's habit of studying and meeting in cafés, are absent.
- **Eight Amman branches** (شارع مكة, الرابية, …) exist only as a string in a chip.
- **Double points for Ramadan and Eid** survive only as a footnote.

The gloom is not caused by black and white. It comes from having no authored marks and a flat hierarchy.

#### 3. Cognitive load: 3 of 8 checks fail (moderate)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Single focus | Pass. The code is one tap from everywhere. |
| Chunking | **Fail.** The hero card holds points, JOD worth, a progress sentence, the gift note, the tier chip, the wallet with «اشحن», and the code button. |
| Grouping | Pass. |
| Visual hierarchy | Pass above the fold; flat below it. |
| One thing at a time | Pass. Redeem asks for an amount, then a confirmation. |
| Minimal choices | **Fail.** |
| Working memory | Pass, with a small bridge in the tier criteria. |
| Progressive disclosure | **Fail.** Every customization group on the item sheet is open at once. |

Decision points with more than 4 options:
- **Item sheet:** «نكهة القهوة» has 16 chips, «نوع الحليب» 8, extras at least 4. That is about 32 choices, with no defaults surfaced.
- **Cart:** «طريقة الدفع» has 6 radio buttons, plus a wallet upsell row.
- **Order tab:** 4 sub-tabs, 12 trending circles and about 33 category chips all sit above the first product.
- **Home carousel:** 6 slides.
- **Home code entry:** three separate ways in (header QR, the card's button, the centre tab), plus the sheet that opens itself on launch.

#### 4. Emotional journey (peak-end rule)

- **Open the app.** The splash with the logo is the best brand moment in the app. Then a QR sheet covers Home, then a black card of numbers. The first feeling is a bank statement, not a café. Valley.
- **Order.** The item sheet opens on a large doodle-cup photo, a small peak. Then comes a wall of chips (valley). The cart turns photographs into glyphs. «ستكسب 50 نقطة» sits in a grey pill. The confirm screen springs a check mark with «وصلنا طلبك» but does not mention points. This is the only animated peak, and it is disconnected from loyalty.
- **Pay and earn.** On a real scan the success badge is a green circle holding the `navigation` arrow icon, not a check mark (EarnQr.tsx). The points arrive at the next launch in a sheet that immediately asks for a star rating, so the peak becomes a chore. The selected stars are grey (#5C5C58).
- **Redeem.** You tap «1.000 د.أ», confirm, and get «تمّ», one sentence and «حسناً». The moment points become money is a system dialog.
- **Tier reached.** A black text banner with an ✕.

Every loop ends on its flattest frame. The missing peaks are: points earned (live, with a count-up), redemption done, a tier crossed, the first order, and a double-points day.

#### 5. Strengths

1. **The code at the counter is done right.** It is one tap from anywhere, the screen goes to full brightness, the token is signed and lasts 60 seconds, and the expired and offline copy is humane, offering the member number as a fallback.
2. **Honest translation of value.** «تساوي 12.400 د.أ، وتكفي ثمن 3 ايس سبانيش لاتيه», redemption in any amount, and a confirmation before it happens.
3. **The photography and the bilingual craft.** Product shots on the branded doodle cups, the item sheet opening on the photo, RTL that is consistently correct (I verified the onboarding pager), Latin digits throughout, and a dark mode that is designed rather than inverted (photos are dimmed).

#### 6. Priority issues

**[P1] No brand illustration or voice: stock icons stand in for character**
- **Why it matters:** this is exactly the owner's "feels AI" complaint, and no colour will fix it on its own.
- **Fix:** answer the owner's open question with yes. Draw an app illustration set in the logo's line weight, using the four logo drawings and the cup doodles. Use it for onboarding, empty cart and vouchers, nudges, the post-visit sheet, the redemption result and the tier ladder. Keep Lucide only for utility glyphs (chevron, close, search). Give tiers words, with the rate as a subtitle. Rewrite the copy in a warm Jordanian register.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable bolder

**[P1] The reward moments are flat**
- **Why it matters:** the peak-end rule decides how people remember the programme, and the programme is the point of the app.
- **Fix:**
  - Post-visit: lead with a large «+15» in the accent, count the balance up from the old figure, and put the rating second and optional.
  - Redemption done: turn the sheet into a voucher stub («خصم 1.000 د.أ») with «اعرض الباركود» as the next step.
  - Tier crossed: a full-width moment that animates the ladder.
  - Confirm screen: show «+50 نقطة».
  - Add `expo-haptics`; it is unused anywhere today.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable delight, then /impeccable animate

**[P1] Monochrome flattens meaning, and urgent value is hidden**
- **What:** 1,000 points (10 JD) expiring on 26 October appear as the last grey nudge, below the fold on Home, and as a muted caption on Rewards.
- **Fix:** let the warm accent own the points system only (see section 8). Make the expiry nudge an accent-edged banner directly under the card, with «استخدمها الآن». Give Home one full-bleed, photo-led order tile instead of five equal slabs.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable colorize

**[P2] The item sheet is a wall of options with a weak selected state**
- **What:** about 32 chips are visible. The selected fill (#E6E6E2) against the resting fill (#F4F4F2) is 1.14:1, with no check mark. Selection is shown five different ways: black fill (category chips), an ink edge (size and milk), green fill (pairings), an underline (sub-tabs) and grey fill (order type).
- **Fix:** collapse each group into a row that shows the current choice («الحليب: بقري طازج ›») and opens a sub-sheet. Show the 4 most popular flavours plus «المزيد». Use one selected style everywhere: ink fill with a check mark.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

**[P2] The hero card overloads the money model**
- **What:** «12.400» (points as JOD) sits next to «12.500» (wallet). The card says «×2» while the tier says «4%». It says «spend 8 JD» while the tier card says «4 visits».
- **Fix:** keep one balance, its worth and the code button. Replace the progress sentence with a thin accent bar and one line («زيارتان وتصبح 4%»). Move the wallet to a quieter row or to Pay.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

#### 7. Persona red flags

**Casey (one-handed, distracted):**
- The combo slide is fully pressable, so a thumb swiping the carousel can drop an americano and a muffin into the cart and jump to it.
- The launch QR sheet covers Home; its ✕ is top-left, the hardest reach for a right thumb.
- The branch chip is 40pt tall, under the 44pt minimum.
- On a slow network the photo plates render empty.
- The item sheet needs about 3 screens of scrolling past chips.

**Jordan (first-timer):**
- The «2% ☕» chip is unexplained.
- «×2», «كاش باك لغاية 9%» (9% matches no tier) and the «BR» badge on brunch tiles are all unexplained.
- Three QR entry points make it unclear whether they are different things.
- Points worth «12.400 د.أ» against wallet «12.500 د.أ»: which money can be spent where?
- The empty «قسائمي» page offers no action and does not say what a voucher is.
- The pickup icon is a pair of footprints.
- Onboarding never shows a drink or a shop.

**Riley (stress tester):**
- The ledger does not add up: +240 and +15 against a balance of 1,240 (mock seed, but it is the demo the owner judges).
- «200 نقطة هدية مع أول شراء» is still pending while the history shows an order.
- «1 زيارات» (not pluralised).
- The scan-success icon is an arrow, not a check mark.
- The barcode button's focused colour is identical to its resting colour in light mode (`fabFocused` uses the same #111111).
- Default payment is «نقداً» even when the wallet covers the order and pays 3% instead of 2%.

#### 8. The one warm accent

**What it means:** black is the shop; the accent is what the shop gives back. Use it only for the points system.

**Where it goes:**
1. **Home card:** the progress bar toward the next rate, plus the bean glyph beside «نقاطك». It means "you're growing".
2. **Earning:** «+15» in `/loyalty` (move it off success green), the cart's «ستكسب 50 نقطة» chip, and «+50 نقطة» on the confirm screen. It means "earned".
3. **Reward moments:** the «+N» figure on the post-visit sheet, the redemption voucher stub, and the selected rating stars. It means "reward".
4. **Tier:** the current-rate badge, the fill of the `/tiers` ladder, and the rate in the tier-up banner. It means "status".
5. **The expiring-points banner** (accent edge and icon). It means "your points need you".

**Where it must not go:** primary buttons (أضف للسلة, مراجعة الطلب, اعرض الباركود stay ink; orange call-to-action buttons would read as Talabat), the tab bar and active states, selected chips, prices (money you pay is not money you earn), links, red error and green system success, large fills or the hero card, the QR code, product plates and body text.

**Contrast rules from the computed ratios:**
- In light mode, an accent fill takes white text. #111111 on any of the candidates is only 3.0–3.4:1.
- In dark mode, an accent fill takes #0F0F0F text. White on any candidate is only 2.1–2.6:1.
- On the black hero card, use the dark-mode value: the light values reach only about 3.2:1 there, which is enough for a bar or other non-text mark but not for text.

**Computed ratios:**

- **Roast** is the liveliest but sits closest to the error red and to Talabat's orange.
- **Brass** is the most distinct from red, but it may read as the "gold" theme the owner already deleted.
- **Rejected: terracotta #B0472E.** Its ΔE from the error red is 0.05, so every «+15» would look like an error.

#### 9. Minor observations

- Photos load into blank white plates with no placeholder.
- The QR card itself pulses (scale 1 to 1.04); pulse the status dot instead, not the surface the cashier scans.
- In dark mode the light photo plates are the brightest slabs on Home.
- The search field draws a rectangular focus ring inside its pill shape.
- The menu cards leave dead space between the name and the price.
- The greeting switches to «مساء الخير» at exactly 12:00.
- Green carries promo copy («اكسب نقاطك مع كل طرق الدفع»); red carries the favourite heart.

#### Questions

- What if the tiers had names drawn from the shop, with the percentage as the subtitle?
- Where are the music and the books from the logo? Could Home show today's barista pick or a branch photo instead of opening on a balance?
- Should the barcode open itself on every launch, or only when the phone is near a branch?
- Could the page be warm paper instead of #FFFFFF and still be "black and white"?
- If the doodles are already printed on every cup, why does the app draw anything else?

**Notable findings**
- **Palette is fully neutral.**
  - Across the light screens there are 9 colours plus a 45 % #111 backdrop: #111111 (945 uses), #ffffff (858), #5c5c58 (311), #f4f4f2 (77), #8a8a85 (73), #efefec (18), #bdbdb8 (10), #e6e6e2 (6), and #000000 (2, QR code only).
  - Dark mode uses #0f0f0f, #1a1a19, #242423, #2a2a28, #3a3a37, #75756f, #a3a39e, #f2f2f0 and #ffffff.
  - Every grey sits at hue 60° with saturation between 1 and 9 %.
  - Colour appears only inside photos: 17.3 % of photo pixels on Home, 6.4 % on Order, 10.1 % in the sheet.
- **The browser is faking bold on 10 text nodes.**

**Possible false positives**
- The QR code is intentionally #000 on #fff. I counted it as a large SVG, not an icon.
- Photos are excluded from the palette. React Native Web paints them as background-image divs; I used the box of the transparent `<img>` behind each one.
- The 67 placeholder pictograms on Order (44 px) count as icons, and the 34 px brand-badge PNG counts as an `<img>`.
- Two invisible, zero-size fixed divs (safe-area listeners) carry a `0.05s` padding transition. I excluded them.
- Of the 9 images counted in Order's first viewport, 2 sit mostly under the floating cart bar. The hit-test figure (22.4 %) accounts for that.
- The sheet's 91.1 % is because #e6e6e2 (saturation 7.4 %, lightness 89.4 %) narrowly misses the white/grey cut-off.
- Dark-mode shadows exist in the CSS (4–7 % black on #0f0f0f) but are effectively invisible.
- "Visually centred" is a heuristic.
- Home's counts include its off-screen carousel slides; its photo coverage does not.
