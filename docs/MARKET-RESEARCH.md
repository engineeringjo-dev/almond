# بحث السوق والمنافسين — ألموند كوفي هاوس

> تقرير بحث معمّق (fan-out على الويب مع تحقّق عدائي من الادّعاءات). **ملاحظة منهجية:** نجت فقط الادّعاءات المدعومة بمصادر أولية متعددة؛ ما لم يُتحقّق منه مُدرج صراحةً في قسم «فجوات لم تُتحقّق» ويجب معاملته كمفتوح.

## الخلاصة
Only 8 of the report's claims survived adversarial verification, and they cluster into two clean evidence-backed pillars: (a) the mechanics of Starbucks' reimagined 2026 loyalty program, which give Almond a concrete, primary-sourced template for tier thresholds, graduated earn rates, expiry-based retention levers, low-friction recurring rewards, and experiential top-tier benefits; and (b) the economics of Middle East food delivery, where incumbent aggregators charge restaurants commissions as high as 30-35% per order, a rate widely viewed as unsustainable and challenged by Noon Food's 15% cap, rooted in structurally weak point-to-point, peak-concentrated unit economics. These findings directly inform Almond's loyalty design (benchmark structure, not JOD amounts) and its channel strategy (own-channel ordering avoids punitive aggregator take rates). Note that the verified evidence base does NOT cover the Amman competitor landscape (Q1), Jordan-specific Talabat/Careem behavior and conversion, search-demand/keyword data (Q3), or Jordan-specific loyalty preferences — these parts of the brief remain unsupported by surviving claims and should be treated as open. All quantitative loyalty figures are US/dollar-denominated and must be re-benchmarked for JOD.

## النتائج المُتحقَّقة

### 1. Starbucks' reimagined 2026 Rewards program provides a benchmarkable three-tier, spend-based ladder with graduated earn rates that reward higher spend.
- **الثقة:** high · **التصويت:** 3-0 (merged from two unanimous claims)
- **الدليل:** Green (entry) earns 1 Star/$; Gold (reached at 500 Stars earned in a 12-month period) earns 1.2 Stars/$ — a 20% improvement over Green; Reserve (reached at 2,500 Stars/year) earns 1.7 Stars/$ (17 Stars per $10). Primary Starbucks press release plus multiple independent outlets confirm identical figures. Thresholds are US/$-denominated, so Almond should benchmark the structure — tiered ladder with escalating earn multipliers — not the exact numbers, in JOD.
- **المصادر:** https://about.starbucks.com/press/2026/starbucks-unveils-reimagined-loyalty-program-to-deliver-more-meaningful-value-personalization-and-engagement-to-members/, https://investor.starbucks.com, https://www.cnbc.com

### 2. Points expiry functions as a deliberate retention lever, with expiry relaxed as members climb tiers.
- **الثقة:** high · **التصويت:** 3-0
- **الدليل:** Green members' Stars expire after six months but can be extended one month via a monthly qualifying activity (a purchase, reward redemption, or card reload); Gold and Reserve members get Stars that never expire. This creates a recurring nudge to transact monthly at the entry tier and rewards loyalty with the removal of expiry pressure. Corroborated by the primary release, investor site, ABC News, and the Rewards Terms of Use.
- **المصادر:** https://about.starbucks.com/press/2026/starbucks-unveils-reimagined-loyalty-program-to-deliver-more-meaningful-value-personalization-and-engagement-to-members/, https://investor.starbucks.com

### 3. Low-threshold and recurring redemption/engagement mechanics drive frequent, low-friction interaction.
- **الثقة:** high · **التصويت:** 3-0
- **الدليل:** A new 60-Star redemption tier ($2 off any purchase) became the most popular redemption at >25% of all redemptions; 'Free Mod Mondays' gives one free beverage modification (up to $2) on one Monday per month to every member regardless of tier; a birthday reward has a redemption window that widens by tier (Green standard, Gold 7 days, Reserve 30 days). These low rungs and recurring hooks maximize redemption frequency. For Almond, an equivalent is a small, easily reachable points reward plus a recurring monthly perk.
- **المصادر:** https://about.starbucks.com/press/2026/starbucks-unveils-reimagined-loyalty-program-to-deliver-more-meaningful-value-personalization-and-engagement-to-members/, https://www.cnbc.com

### 4. The top tier leans on experiential and exclusive-access rewards rather than pure discount value.
- **الثقة:** high · **التصويت:** 3-0
- **الدليل:** Reserve members receive a free personalized physical card, early/first access to the new online Starbucks Shop merchandise store, curated events, and all-expenses-paid coffee-origin trips (Tokyo, Milan, Costa Rica; first trip fall 2026). This signals that Almond's top tier can differentiate on status and experience (e.g., roastery events, exclusive beans/merch, origin storytelling) rather than only richer discounts.
- **المصادر:** https://about.starbucks.com/press/2026/starbucks-unveils-reimagined-loyalty-program-to-deliver-more-meaningful-value-personalization-and-engagement-to-members/, https://www.nrn.com

### 5. Middle East restaurants pay food-delivery aggregators commissions as high as 30-35% per order, which they consider unsustainable.
- **الثقة:** medium · **التصويت:** 3-0
- **الدليل:** MenaBytes reports commissions 'up to 30-35%' with restaurants 'feeling stuck.' Corroborated by Wamda ('unsustainable nature of food delivery'), Nexara ('Why Jordanian Restaurants Pay 30% to Talabat'), and Talabat business-model breakdowns citing 15-35% ranges. Caveat: 30-35% is the ceiling; Jordan-specific typical rates are closer to 15-25%, and exclusive/discount programs can lower them. Secondary sources, but multiply corroborated. This is the core economic case for Almond investing in its own ordering channel.
- **المصادر:** https://www.menabytes.com/food-delivery-wars-middle-east/, https://www.wamda.com, https://nexara.io

### 6. Noon Food entered by capping commission near 15% — roughly half incumbents' 30-35% — and challenged rivals to match it.
- **الثقة:** medium · **التصويت:** 3-0
- **الدليل:** In Feb 2021 Noon (Mohamed Alabbar) launched with a 15% commission cap, publicly challenging incumbents charging 30-35% during COVID to match it; Careem responded with a 0% variable-commission model. Minor imprecision: Noon's effective total was ~17% (15% + 2% payment handling). This is a documented historical market event, not a current live rate — verify present-day aggregator terms before relying on it operationally.
- **المصادر:** https://www.menabytes.com/food-delivery-wars-middle-east/, https://www.arabnews.com, https://www.thenationalnews.com

### 7. Food-delivery unit economics are structurally weak due to point-to-point trips and peak-concentrated demand.
- **الثقة:** high · **التصويت:** 3-0
- **الدليل:** Each order requires a dedicated one-supplier-to-one-customer trip, and demand concentrates at lunch/dinner, leaving drivers idle most of the day. Independently corroborated by McKinsey and operations-research literature (off-peak demand ~5% of peak). This is a structural/timeless mechanism explaining why aggregator take rates are high — and why batching only partially mitigates it. It reinforces the strategic logic for Almond to favor pickup/own-channel where feasible.
- **المصادر:** https://www.menabytes.com/food-delivery-wars-middle-east/, https://www.mckinsey.com

## تحفّظات
Scope gap (most important): The 8 surviving claims cover ONLY loyalty-program mechanics (Starbucks) and food-delivery economics. They do NOT substantiate the report's Amman competitor landscape (Q1: Caffè Strada, Rumi Earth, Shakespeare & Co, Caribou, Costa, Barista, local roasters — positioning, prices, digital presence, loyalty, social following), Jordan-specific Talabat/Careem customer behavior and conversion drivers (part of Q2), search-demand/keyword and Google Trends data (Q3), or any Jordan-specific loyalty preference evidence (part of Q4). Those sections of the brief must be treated as unverified. Currency/geography: All Starbucks figures are US/$-denominated; Almond operates in JOD and Jordan, so every threshold, earn rate, and reward value is a structural benchmark only, not a transferable number. Time-sensitivity: The Starbucks program launches March 10, 2026 and is very fresh — details could shift; the Noon/Careem commission events are from 2021 and current aggregator terms in Jordan should be independently reconfirmed. Source quality: Loyalty claims rest on a primary Starbucks press release (strong, though the direct URL returned HTTP 403 and was verified via search-index mirrors and independent outlets); delivery-economics claims rest on secondary trade press (MenaBytes, Wamda, Arab News) — credible but not primary, hence medium confidence on the commission figures. Two related claims were refuted and excluded: that Careem switched to a fixed monthly-fee model, and that aggregators have been unprofitable since inception.
